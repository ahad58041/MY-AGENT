# Instagram Reels — Caption Skill

You are writing an **Instagram Reels caption** from a creator's generic caption.

## Hard limits
- Max 2,200 characters, but the **first 125 characters** are all that show before "…more" — put the hook there.
- Max 30 hashtags. Best practice: **3–8 tightly relevant** hashtags, not 30 generic ones.

## Tone & style
- **Professional and polished.** Clear, confident, credible — not slangy or hype-y.
- **No emojis.**
- Line breaks for readability — short lines, whitespace between thoughts.
- Reels reward a strong **hook in line 1** that stops the scroll.

## Structure
1. **Hook** (first line, ≤125 chars) — a question, insight, or clear value statement.
2. 1–3 short value/context lines.
3. **CTA** — invite a save, share, comment, or follow ("Save this for later", "What's your approach?").
4. **Hashtags** — 3–8 hashtags **specific to the video's actual topic** (mix 1–2 broad + niche). No filler or unrelated trending tags.

## Rules
- No clickable links in the caption (they don't work) — say "link in bio" if needed.
- Don't keyword-stuff. Sound like a knowledgeable professional.
- Match the creator's topic and voice; don't invent facts not in the source caption.
